# 07. Opening Balance

## WHAT
The initial financial position loaded into the chart of accounts at the start of a financial year or upon migration.

## WHY
Enables continuity between accounting periods or historical migration from legacy ERP systems.

## RULES
- Opening balance total debits must equal total credits.
- If there is a disparity between total opening debits and total opening credits, the difference is **shown** (Trial Balance difference; "Difference in Opening Balances" line on the Balance Sheet). It is never posted, never moved to Suspense and never auto-adjusted.
- Income and Expense are period accounts and carry no opening balance; Balance Sheet positions (incl. provisions/reserves) do. A new year opens with the previous year closing positions, including the previous years' profit/loss (Reserves & Surplus) and stock.

## WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE
- The mandate that unbalanced opening balances must be explicitly shown rather than hidden or auto-adjusted.
